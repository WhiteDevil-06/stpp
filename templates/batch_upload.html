CSV File Guidelines & Specifications
Required Columns
  • title - Task title/name
  • deadline (or deadline_date) - Format YYYY-MM-DD
  • urgency - Numeric scale 1 to 10
  • business_impact - Numeric scale 1 to 10
  • estimated_effort - Estimated hours (numeric > 0)
  • dependency_count - Number of dependencies (>= 0)
  • task_type (optional) - e.g., Development, Bug, Feature, General
Processing Rules & Limits
  • Maximum limit: 500 tasks per upload.
  • Invalid rows (missing fields, past dates, out-of-range values) will be detailed in the error summary.
  • Valid tasks will be processed using the Decision Tree model and rule engine.
  • Results can be previewed online and downloaded as a formatted CSV file.
